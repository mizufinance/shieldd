# Compliance Ciphertext Soundness Properties

The `ID` column is the stable key checked by
`scripts/check-soundness-invariants.sh`. “Research-only” DLEQ rows do not
contribute to deployed Transfer certification.

| `SECRECY` | No public tier-seed opener. | V20 serializes EPK, c2, ciphertext, two core key confirmations, and factored metadata; it contains no subject derivation, per-ciphertext ACK, PRE envelope, DH shared point, DLEQ proof, or equivalent seed-opening field. A confirmation is testable only after recovering the candidate tier seed. For a fresh per-action CSPRNG nonce root and honestly derived, nonzero, undisclosed tier randomness, computational plaintext secrecy is conditional on the Decaf377 and Poseidon-stream assumptions. The root must not be reused by another Transfer or fee funding in the enclosing transaction. Intentional disclosure by the action creator is out of scope because the circuit cannot prove entropy, uniqueness, or non-collusion. | `enrich_plan_with_compliance`; `build_transfer_compliance`; `sample_nonzero_scalar`; `TransferOutputBody`; `TransferComplianceCiphertext`; `ComplianceEvidenceObject`; `encrypt_transfer`; `encrypt_tier_bytes` | A non-colluding observer recovers a tier seed or plaintext solely from honestly generated accepted public bytes. | `assumed` |
