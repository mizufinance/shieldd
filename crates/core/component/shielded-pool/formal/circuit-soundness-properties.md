| `WITNESS-ABI-CANONICALITY` | all gnark-backed circuit families | Every accepted witness byte string has one typed interpretation: booleans are 0/1, tree paths have exact depth/arity, field and point encodings are canonical, Fr scalars are below the companion order, and withdrawal effect limbs are 128-bit. | The circuit still enforces semantic ranges such as 128-bit amounts and exact path positions. | Shared Rust/Go binary decoders reject aliases before assignment; the receipt hashes the exact accepted witness. | Prover or build tooling changes bytes without changing the reduced circuit assignment, weakening artifact receipts or creating cross-language parser disagreement. | `composed` |
| `FIXED-ARITY-PRIVACY` | transfer, NoteReshape, shielded ICS-20 withdrawal | Public action bodies do not reveal synthetic slots through special-length or all-zero encryption sentinels. | Dummy selectors, zero-value constraints, synthetic nullifiers, and disabled membership remain private circuit facts. | Synthetic slots use ordinary fixed-length backreference and note-key encryption; observers and consensus process all fixed slots uniformly. | Observer classifies the real input/output arity without breaking encryption. | `composed` |
| `ACTION-ANCHOR-CONSISTENCY` | transfer, NoteReshape, shielded ICS-20 withdrawal | The retained action-body anchor must equal the single transaction anchor used as the proof's public membership root. | Circuit statement binds the transaction-context anchor. | Stateless extraction rejects a body-anchor copy that differs from `TransactionContext.anchor`; stateful validation checks the transaction anchor. | Submitter mutates an effect-hash-exempt body anchor or creates two apparent anchor claims for one action. | `composed` |
| `CONSENSUS-PROOF-EXTRACTION-COMPOSITION` | transfer, NoteReshape, shielded ICS-20 withdrawal, fee funding | A proof artifact may enter proposal aggregation only as the result of the same successful stateless validation that checks its action anchor, authorization signatures, proof transport, family shape, and public statement. | Each circuit constrains the proof relation and claimed statement hash. | `collect_consensus_proof_items_with_artifacts` invokes the combined validation/extraction helper for every shielded action; only those combined helpers are publicly exported, while raw extraction and batch-item construction remain crate-private. | `OUTGOING-VIEW-BINDING` | transfer, NoteReshape, shielded ICS-20 withdrawal | Every output's outgoing-view key is wrapped under the serialized aggregate action balance commitment, including synthetic padding and withdrawal change. | The circuit constrains the aggregate balance commitment to the complete input/output value relation. | Action construction passes the same aggregate commitment stored in the body to every `Note::encrypt_key` call; transaction payload-key recovery unwraps with that body commitment. | `PLAN-BOUNDARY-CANONICALITY` | transfer, shielded ICS-20 withdrawal | Every admitted or constructed shielded plan has a required canonical transaction blinding nonce, fixed-width canonical Merkle paths, and mutually consistent anchors, timestamps, asset/compliance facts, payload, balance commitment, spends, and outputs.
